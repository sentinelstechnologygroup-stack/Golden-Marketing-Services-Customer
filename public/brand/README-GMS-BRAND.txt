IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
1. Use the horizontal logo on dark/navy backgrounds.
2. Use the icon-only mark for favicon, app icon, social avatar, and compact mobile placements.
3. Do not redraw, stretch, recolor, rotate, or alter the mountain/arrow emblem.
4. Keep sufficient clear space around the logo; avoid placing it directly against navigation text.
5. Primary tagline belongs with the corporate brand. The secondary line is optional campaign/supporting copy.
6. Recommended site background family: deep navy/black. Gold is the primary accent; silver/white is supporting.
7. For smallest favicon sizes, use the supplied pre-rendered files rather than shrinking the full horizontal logo.
