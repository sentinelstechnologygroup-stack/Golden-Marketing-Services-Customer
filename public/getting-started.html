Customer Portal · Quick start

Your portal. One click away.

Finish account setup once, then keep your GMS workspace within easy reach.

Open Customer Portal →

01 · Create your password and sign in

  1. Open the personal setup email from Golden Marketing Services and choose Create my password.
  2. Choose your password, then sign in using the email address your administrator registered.
  3. Review your lead activity, appointments, reports, and program information. Your account’s permissions determine which sections are available.

An expired or already-used link requires a new setup email from your GMS administrator. Never share your password or setup link.

02 · Add your portal to your desktop

Use Chrome or Edge on your computer. Click Install app in the portal and confirm the browser’s installation prompt.

  • Chrome: If you need the menu, use the three dots → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app. This menu also contains sharing tools; you do not need to cast anything.
  • Already seeing “Open in app”? The app is installed. Enter chrome://apps in Chrome’s address bar, right-click the GMS app, select Create shortcut, choose Desktop, and click Create.
  • Edge: Use the three dots → Apps → Install this site as an app. Use the browser’s app-management options to create a shortcut or pin it.
  • iPhone or iPad: Open the portal in Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Your desktop icon opens the portal directly. Internet access and your own account are still required.

03 · Find it from our website

Visit goldenmarketingservices.com. In the footer, find Portal Access and choose Customer Portal. Or bookmark the direct portal link above.

04 · Stay current

The portal checks for updates when opened, when you return to its window, and every minute while open. When Portal update available appears, save your work and finish any active call before selecting Update now.

Sessions end after an hour without activity or at midnight Central time. Sign back in to resume your work.
