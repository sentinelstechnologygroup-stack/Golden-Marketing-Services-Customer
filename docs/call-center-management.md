# GMS staff management and call-center reporting — October 11, 2026

Super admins can edit existing staff identity, work email, role, active/archive status and multiple client/brand/campaign assignments with updateGmsStaff. Fixed role permissions are derived server-side. Phone agents retain least privilege; supervisors gain assigned-client oversight and reporting; only super admins manage staff. Existing Patrick role is not changed by this release.

Archive disables Firebase login, revokes refresh tokens, revokes assignments and disables inbound presence, while retaining call evidence and history. Reactivation restores chosen assignments but starts unavailable. No destructive identity/evidence deletion is offered. Self-demotion/archive and platform-owner demotion are prevented. Access reductions during a busy call are rejected. Account changes require another sign-in. Existing provider credentials are preserved; a newly assigned client can reuse this employee's already configured receive-only phone credential. New employees still use existing phone provisioning.

Selected campaign IDs are checked against tenant brands. Default all-campaign scope preserves prior access. Selected scope filters leads/campaigns and linked tasks, appointments, alerts and call records, and guards transitions, appointment creation, queue claim, call start and inbound acceptance. Direct inquiries without campaign remain covered for assigned brands.

getGmsCallCenterReport verifies fresh Firebase identity. Global admins see GMS clients; supervisors see active assigned brands only. Phone agents are denied. CSV is generated locally from authorized response, with spreadsheet formula injection escaping. No secrets, provider credentials or recording URLs are returned in reports.

Definitions: queue = queue entry to acceptance or queue exit; connection delay = acceptance to conference audio connection; handling = connected to completed handoff or hangup, including hold; hold = successful provider hold/unhold intervals; peak concurrency = connected GMS-managed calls, excluding post-handoff conversation. Break/lunch/after-call status intervals are clipped to report bounds; stale current presence stops at last heartbeat plus 45 seconds. Status activity is operational evidence, not payroll. Dates are entered in browser local timezone; day grouping uses Central time.

New timing timestamps begin at deployment. Legacy missing timings show Not collected and are excluded from timing averages. Voicemail recording duration is not queue wait. Current status counts derive authoritative assignment documents, not stale mirror records. Archived staff names/history remain visible in reports. Reports bound periods to 93 days, 2,000 calls per client and 10,000 status transitions; capped results are explicitly incomplete. Query throughput limits mean this is an initial reporting foundation, not a complete workforce-management suite. Current coverage refreshes when running the report.

Verification: staff creation/edit role authorization, archive behavior, foreign campaign rejection, active-call guard, role claim preservation, report access/bounds, queue/voicemail timing, concurrency and status clipping, idempotent hold measurement, inbound and provider adapter regression tests. No real staff promotion/archive, outgoing email, SMS or phone call was performed for verification.

## Expanded operations reports (October 11)
- Answer speed uses queue entry to confirmed audio connection for answered inbound calls only. The acceptance-to-audio measure remains separate.
- Queue abandonment requires an ended queued inbound call with no acceptance, connection, voicemail, or callback request. The denominator is ended calls with observed queue entry. Missing historical queue timestamps are not assumed to mean zero wait or zero abandonment.
- Failed agent connections require an accepted call that ended without a confirmed connection. They identify a technical connection outcome, not employee fault. Shared unanswered alerts are never assigned as a miss to every eligible agent.
- Transfer reports separate completed, failed/unresolved, cancelled, and pending outcomes. An unresolved provider state is not proof of employee error.
- Qualification attribution is server-recorded on first qualification. Historic qualifications without qualifying-agent evidence stay unattributed; current ownership is not substituted. The qualified-lead call breakdown deduplicates contacted leads within each group.
- Callback overdue reporting includes open tasks due by the report end, including carryover tasks. Explicit completion time and completing agent are recorded when staff complete a task; missing historical completion timestamps are excluded from timing averages. Calling a contact alone does not silently complete a task.
- Campaign, daily, and Monday-based Central weekly breakdowns use the selected report population. Partial weeks are not normalized to full weeks.
- Agent session idle timeout is deferred while a healthy connected phone is Available or a call is active. Coverage must have a successful presence/queue check within 45 seconds. Standby, breaks, lunch, do-not-disturb, and offline retain the idle timeout; Standby remains unavailable for inbound calls. Idle protection refreshes the activity date during an on-duty overnight shift.

## Callback and voicemail workspace
- Agent Workspace is the working home. Each callback/follow-up item has an explicit Mark completed action; changing call notes does not complete a task. Successful completion refreshes counts. Callback completion also clears a linked voicemail from the waiting list, while the contact retains the complete history and recording.
- Work panels refresh every twenty seconds and on focus. Callback/follow-up New badges acknowledge opened items on this device only; they are not completed-work metrics. Voicemail unheard status is server-recorded only when audio begins playing. Opening a contact or downloading audio does not mark it listened.
- Waiting ages use received timestamps, separately from due dates. Missing historical dates show Not collected. Call history and task panels have bounded scrolling so they do not grow indefinitely above the CRM conversation.
- Approved GCR provider retention: remove the Telnyx recording after re-reading and hashing both independent GMS archives and verifying Telnyx recording/call ownership. Confirm removal with a subsequent lookup. GMS files are not deleted. Retention/legal holds remain respected. The archive worker retries cleanup after archive completion without re-downloading provider media. Transcription uses the retained GMS archive and does not require retaining the Telnyx copy.
